🛟 Quick safety tips

  • Move to higher ground early; do not wait for water to enter the house.
  • Switch off the main power and gas if it is safe to reach the switch.
  • Take your emergency bag, documents and medicines.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions and use the routes you are told to use.
  • Keep children and pets with you.
  • Do not walk, swim or drive through floodwater – it can be deeper and faster than it looks.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
  • Get out first, then call 999. Stay out.
  • Stay low if there is smoke and feel closed doors with the back of your hand before opening.
  • Use stairs, never lifts.
  • If clothes catch fire: stop, drop and roll.
  • Do not go back inside for belongings.
  • Do not use water on cooking-oil or electrical fires.
  • Warning signs: new cracks in the ground or walls, tilting trees or poles, muddy streams, unusual rumbling.
  • If you see signs, leave the area immediately, moving sideways away from the slope path.
  • Report it – DOE line above, or 999 if people are in danger.
  • Stay away from the slope after the slide: more ground may move.
  • Do not stay near a slope during or just after heavy, prolonged rain.
